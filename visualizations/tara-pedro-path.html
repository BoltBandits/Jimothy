What to look for

Curve 1: the robot turns from 90° to 180° as it travels from Start to Join.

Curve 2: reverseTangent() points the robot opposite the curve's direction of travel. It still travels from Join to End.

Dashed lines and hollow squares are shaping handles. The robot does not have to pass through those control points.
